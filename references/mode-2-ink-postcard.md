# Mode 2 — Ivory Ink-Study Postcard · 米白画册水墨明信片

## Layout

Create one vertical matte-ivory art-book postcard, preferably 3:4. Divide it visually into an **upper faithful photograph** and **lower interpretive ink study**. Do not impose the Mode 4 physical-interaction conceit here.

## Upper: authentic photograph

- Retain the original subject, perspective, buildings, water, vegetation, human pose, spatial relationships, native low-saturation colors and original daylight.
- Only crop proportionally if necessary. Do not restage, beautify, repaint, apply a film filter or change objects.
- If literal pixel fidelity is essential, composite the original photo as a deterministic unchanged crop rather than asking an image generator to recreate it.

## Lower: distilled reconstruction

- Place a minimal re-interpretation of all important upper-scene components within generous warm-ivory paper space.
- Collapse the photographed environment into quiet layers of geometric but soft-edged color masses: foreground, middle ground, horizon, silhouettes, architectural volumes and negative spaces.
- Apply light brush-ink diffusion, restrained pale ink washes and subdued matte flat tones. Preserve palette relationships faithfully.
- Remove busy textures, individual foliage, clutter and complicated reflections, but preserve the **spatial essence** of the source.
- No hard vector contours, neon fills, photo filters, exaggerated lighting or unrelated objects.

## Typography

At the bottom center:
1. One elegant English title using handwritten-feeling serif lettering.
2. One smaller English descriptive sentence in clean sans-serif.

Generate understated source-grounded English text if not specified by the user. Maintain breathing room and legibility. Do not turn the work into a marketing flyer.

## Visual finish

Matte special-paper tactility, restrained Eastern landscape/architecture research-book sensibility, warm off-white, high negative-space ratio, quiet editorial scholarship. No stickers, decorative badges, additional borders, glossy gradients, brand graphics or watermarks.

## Prompt template

Create a portrait 3:4 matte warm-ivory postcard. Upper portion: faithfully preserve the actual input photograph [name specific foreground, midground, subject, lighting and color], only proportionally crop; no changes to original physical structure or mood. Lower portion: in a separate spacious warm paper field, reconstruct those same shapes as soft muted geometric ink-wash color layers, preserving [source-specific palette and spatial rhythm] while simplifying all small detail. Add at bottom center a delicate handwritten-style English serif title "[title]" and a smaller sans-serif line "[caption]". Restrained East Asian art-book landscape study; no busy patterns, sharp vector outlines, marketing graphics, invented scenery or altered photographic light.
