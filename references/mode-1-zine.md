# Mode 1 — Scenes Gathered Zine · 场景撕口拼贴

## Intended result

Treat the real photo as an authentic spatial anchor. A tactile, irregular threshold opens into an illustration built from **the same scene**, using the photographed geometry to determine edges, negative space and transitions. Not a photo rectangle placed over wallpaper.

## Preflight decisions

1. Name the story center: person/action, waterline, coastal curve, roof cluster, a single tree, architectural rhythm, etc.
2. Select the minimum photographic material needed to retain its identity and depth. Keeping only a person, an edge of water, or part of the skyline is allowed. **Never prescribe 35%, 50%, or another universal photo fraction.**
3. Trace a natural structural route for the material handoff: coastline, waterline, crown of trees, roof profile, street geometry, ridge, or another real contour.
4. In illustration space, repeat the **structure** of source components (foreground/midground/background, directions, relative heights), not a literal cut-and-paste copy of every detail.
5. Decide on a single uninterrupted hue, contour, water movement, light path, or directional gesture that connects photo to illustration.

## Materials and transitions

- Tactile hand-torn/fibrous paper edge, locally irregular in ways explained by photographed forms. It can be partially invisible where imagery crosses the edge.
- Immediately next to the edge, preserve the photograph's native color impression; only then decay gradually into restrained halftone, dry brush or silkscreen texture.
- Illustration is source-dependent, not disconnected decorative wallpaper. Keep clear blocks, purposeful quiet space and breathing room.
- The handoff must make spatial sense (water can flow through, a road can continue, a canopy can transform into an abstract shape).

## Invariants

- No unrelated architecture, people, foliage, skyline or fantasy props.
- No default rectangular photo sticker, uniform white stroke, hard digital masking or generic ragged-edge filter.
- No excess texture and no global recoloring of the protected photographic anchor.
- If letterforms are requested, keep them small and subordinate, without introducing a magazine headline template by default.

## Prompt plan

Retain [source-specific focal subject] as authentic photography; keep [protected spatial relationships/colors]. Let the torn aperture follow [source contour]. Reinterpret [source elements] outside the photo as [simplified illustration masses]. Preserve source hues near the torn edge, fading into [halftone/dry-brush/silkscreen]. Carry [one scene-derived hue or directional gesture] continuously across both media. Keep substantial purposeful paper negative space; no rectangular sticker, uniform border, generic digital mask or unrelated objects.
