# Mode 3 — Surreal Matte Pop Collage · 超现实平涂拼贴

## Core

Produce a 3:4 vertical surreal pop collage with **the actual input subject clearly recognizable** and its **original colors protected**. Prioritize a bold, simple impossible scale relationship over complicated layering.

## Source-first choices

1. Identify the main source subject and specific colors that must remain intact.
2. Honor subject/body/prop constraints, such as `hands empty` or `no props`.
3. Select **exactly one** original photographed item to enlarge into an impossible singular giant. It must have existed in the original image and remain recognizable.
4. Replace the background with **2–3 giant flat matte irregular color fields**; name their 2–3 specific colors. Coordinate with source colors instead of recoloring the subject.
5. Select a family of small original-source-related elements. Distribute these in graduated sizes following **one curved arc**. Don't scatter many unrelated species.
6. Add only a few loose white hand-drawn marks, placed as direction cues rather than a decorative blanket.
7. Emphasize flat opaque fills, sharp readable silhouette and editorial negative space.

## Hard negatives

No gradients, drop shadows, simulated studio lighting, words, captions, logos, watermark, multiple giants, uncontrolled props, dramatic photorealistic background, or altered original subject color. No paper-rip + ink postcard hybrid.

## Prompt template

Surreal pop collage, vertical 3:4. Preserve [original source subject] clearly recognizable and preserve its original [specific colors], obeying [user constraints]. Replace the background with two or three matte flat irregular fields: [color A/shape], [color B/shape], [optional color C/shape]. Introduce one impossible large-scale [source-object], and one arc of [source-related small elements] scaling from [large] to [tiny]. Include a few white hand-drawn line marks to support [motion direction]. No gradients, shadows, typography or watermark.
