**模式3：**

**模式4：**

> 使用模式4「第二世界」处理这张有倒影的河边照片。上下严格1:1，整体3:4。不要套撕纸小人模板；请从倒影结构推导一个真正可以互动的第二空间。上半原照不改，下半米白留白。给我先说明构思再出图。

**自动选择模式：**

## Example output (hypothetical, not an actual uploaded image)

- **画面理解**：叙事焦点为桥下倒影；必须保留原桥梁、岸线、色彩和反射方向。
- **设计方案**：把倒影的接触线解读为可以轻轻掀开的入口；下半米白纸张仅有一笔延长的水线与一个小型黑线人物正伸手揭开水面。仅一个动作机制，不再加入其它装饰。

In the lower equal-height panel of warm matte ivory paper, transform the photographed reflection line into a single liftable threshold: one tiny minimal black ink figure gently opens its edge, creating an implied second space. Let the line continue across the photographic boundary. Reserve ample paper white space. Add one scene-specific understated hand-lettered English line, e.g. "It opens from this side." No stock torn-paper effect, repeated figures, stars, photo recoloring or commercial styling.`
